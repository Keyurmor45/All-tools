HOW TO REACH US

AllTools is an independent, indie developer project engineered and maintained by a small team dedicated to creating free, privacy-first software. In line with our anti-bloat philosophy, we do not operate a traditional corporate support email inbox or automated ticketing desk. Instead, we maintain direct, open, and instant communication channels with our community. Here is how you can connect with us:

💬 COMMUNITY WALL (GUESTBOOK)

GO TO WALL ↗

The fastest way to leave feedback, suggest a new tool, or report a bug. Messages appear instantly. No account required.

💡 SUGGEST A NEW TOOL

Have an idea for a web developer utility, text converter, calculation tool, or privacy helper that should be part of AllTools? We regularly review community requests and build new tools based on your feedback.

When suggesting a new tool on The Wall, tell us:

  • Tool Function: What the tool does and its main purpose.
  • Use Case: How you would use it in your daily workflow or projects.
  • Inputs & Outputs: What inputs you expect to provide and what outputs it should generate.

🐛 REPORT A BUG

Found a bug, visual layout issue, or unexpected behavior in any of our 500+ tools? We appreciate detailed bug reports to keep AllTools running flawlessly.

When reporting an issue on The Wall, please include:

  • Location: The exact page or tool ID where the bug occurred.
  • Behavior: What you expected to happen vs. what actually happened.
  • Environment: Your browser name and version (e.g. Chrome 126, Firefox 128, Safari 17) and device OS.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: Can I request a custom tool?

A: Yes! Leave a detailed suggestion on The Wall explaining what the tool should do and how it benefits your workflow.

Q: Is there a premium version?

A: No. AllTools is and will always be completely free for all users with no premium tiers, paywalls, or feature locks.

Q: Can I use AllTools in my own project?

A: AllTools is built for end-users. If you are interested in embedding individual tools or code snippets into your own project, reach out to us via The Wall.

Q: Why is a tool not working?

A: Most issues are caused by cached browser data or conflicting extensions. Try refreshing the page, clearing site data, or testing in an incognito window / different browser. If it still doesn't work, report it on The Wall.

Q: How do I delete my local data?

A: Go to your browser settings → Privacy & Security → Site Data / Storage → find the AllTools domain → Clear Site Data. You can also use the reset buttons available on the Stats and System Settings panels.
